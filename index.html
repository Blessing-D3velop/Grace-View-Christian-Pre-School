About Us

A Place to Belong, Learn and Grow.

At Graceview Christian Pre-School, we believe every child deserves an environment where they feel welcomed, cared for and encouraged to learn. Inspired by our commitment to educating and nurturing young minds, we aim to make every learning experience meaningful, positive and memorable.

Graceview is a Christian pre-school and daycare at 111 Gardiner Street, Brakpan, Johannesburg, with classes for babies, Grade RR and Grade R, plus after care and Saturday care.

Every little mind deserves the opportunity to shine.

Joyce — Owner
